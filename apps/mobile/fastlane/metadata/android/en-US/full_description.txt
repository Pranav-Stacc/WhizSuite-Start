WhizSuite is the mobile command center for modern marketing and creative agencies. Manage clients, content, calendars, tasks, approvals, and your client portal — wherever your team works.

Built for agency owners, account managers, producers, and client-facing portal users.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Clients & CRM — accounts, agreements, and client workspace access
• Content & campaigns — plan, review, and track deliverables
• Calendar & shoots — schedules and production dates in one place
• Team & roles — permissions that match how your agency is organized
• Approvals — keep sign-off flows moving on the go
• Messages — conversations tied to client work
• Invoices & billing visibility — stay aligned with finance (where enabled)
• Notifications — stay on top of what needs attention

WHY AGENCIES CHOOSE WHIZSUITE
• One secure workspace per organization (multi-tenant by design)
• Staff app and client portal experiences in a single product
• Sign in with email or Google — fast access for distributed teams
• Designed for agencies in India and worldwide (INR-friendly defaults)

WhizSuite connects to your WhizSuite cloud workspace. You need an active agency account or invitation to sign in.
